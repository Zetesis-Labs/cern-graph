# The OC5 subsidiary rules

OC5 states principles; the subsidiary rules make them operational, family by family:
**IAA** (identities, authentication, authorization), **SWR** (software restrictions),
**NET** (networking), **EPT** (endpoints), **OPS** (service operations), **DPP**
(data protection and privacy), **DEV** (software development — where mandatory
SecureFlag training for people who touch code lives).
