# The password policy

Aligned with **NIST 800-63b**: complexity requirements dropped, minimum length raised
to **15 characters**, and every new password checked against lists of trivial and
breach-exposed ("burnt") passwords. Non-compliant passwords across every account type
— primary, secondary, service, database, guest — must rotate during 2026.
