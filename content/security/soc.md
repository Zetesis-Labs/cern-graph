# The Security Operations Centre

The SOC ingests and correlates the laboratory's security telemetry — network traffic
plus the logs of external services such as Google Workspace and Microsoft Azure —
looking for the anomalies that a firewall alone will not catch. Its expansion was one
of the concrete outcomes of the [[cis-v8-audit]].
