# Data privacy

OC5 protects the Organization; **OC11 protects the person**. The framework:
[[oc11]] as the internal law, the [[office-of-data-privacy]] as the centre of expertise,
the [[data-protection-commission]] as independent supervisor, and [[dpia]] as the
procedure that puts privacy analysis before deployment.
