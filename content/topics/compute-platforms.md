# Compute platforms

Almost nothing at CERN runs on a machine somebody owns. [[cern-cloud]] provisions over
90% of the data centre; [[batch-service]] turns part of it into a high-throughput pool
that replaced [[lsf]]; [[cern-paas]] hosts the web tier; and [[swan]] gives physicists
a notebook that reaches all of it.
