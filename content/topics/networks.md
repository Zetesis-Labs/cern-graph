# Networks

Moving exabytes between continents does not happen on the public internet.
[[lhcopn]] is the private optical network between CERN and the Tier-1s; [[lhconet]]
extends the model to the Tier-2s; and inside the site, [[network-segregation]] keeps
the accelerator's technical network apart from the campus.
