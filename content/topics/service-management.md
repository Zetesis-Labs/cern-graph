# Service management

The layer that makes 200 services legible as one organisation: [[servicenow]] as the
platform, the [[service-desk]] as the single door, the [[service-catalogue]] as the
map, [[service-management-roles]] as the accountability model, [[itil-processes]] as
the machinery, [[status-boards]] as the public face, [[event-management-gni]] wiring
the monitoring in — and [[ggus]] bridging the grid's own ticketing into all of it.
