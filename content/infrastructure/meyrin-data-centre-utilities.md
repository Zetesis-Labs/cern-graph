# Meyrin Data Centre utilities

The Meyrin computing centre's supporting utilities are a distinct resilience concern:
electrical distribution, cooling, racks and dual power feeds are being modernised for
the High-Luminosity LHC era. This node records the system boundary, not its sensitive
equipment layout.
