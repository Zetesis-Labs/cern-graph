# The Prévessin Computing Centre

Inaugurated on **23 February 2024**: more than 6000 m² on the Prévessin site, six IT
rooms with 2 MW of cooling each. Its **PUE target of 1.1** against a world average
near 1.6 is the design statement, and its heat-recovery system will warm site buildings
from the winter of 2026/27. Energy is treated as an architectural constraint, not an
operating expense.
