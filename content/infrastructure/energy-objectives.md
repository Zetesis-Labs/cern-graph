# The energy objectives

In 2024 the Enlarged Directorate approved environmental objectives to 2030, committing
CERN to limit electricity consumption to **1.5 TWh/year during LHC Run 4**. With
ISO 50001 certification and a decade of measures that already cut consumption by ~10%,
the ceiling turns "more computing" into a trade-off rather than a given — the
constraint under which HL-LHC computing models must be designed.
