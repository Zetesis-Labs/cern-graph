# The Meyrin Data Centre

The historical heart of CERN computing and the site of the WLCG **Tier-0** obligation,
now working alongside the newer [[prevessin-computing-centre]]. Two centres rather than
one is not redundancy for its own sake: it is what lets capacity be added, and hardware
retired, without interrupting an experiment that runs for years at a time.
