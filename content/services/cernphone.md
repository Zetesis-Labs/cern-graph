# CERNphone

The telephony service: a **dedicated SIP backend** with provisioning interfaces and two
clients — **CERNphone mobile**, built on the open-source **Linphone** stack and
customised for CERN, and **CERNphone desktop**, developed in-house for Windows, macOS
and Linux. It retired both **Skype for Business** (2013–2021) and the **Alcatel IP
phones** (2006–2014), with the ageing Alcatel exchange handed over progressively to
CERN's own TONE system. The clients are developed in the open on GitHub.

It is the [[malt]] logic applied to the phone system: rather than replacing one vendor
with another, build on open protocols and keep the backend at home.
