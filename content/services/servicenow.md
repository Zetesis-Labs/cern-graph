# ServiceNow at CERN

Live since **February 2011**, and organisation-wide from birth: service management came
in as a **joint project of the General Services and IT departments**, so the platform
never was an IT tool that later escaped — snow clearing, car parking, drainage,
employee onboarding and failed lightbulbs ride the same processes as a broken mail
account. Between 2011 and 2018 the yearly ticket volume **multiplied by five**.

Around it, the pieces this hub maps: the [[service-catalogue]] it implements, the
[[service-desk]] answering one number for everything, the [[itil-processes]] chosen and
deliberately simplified for adoption, the [[service-management-roles|accountability
model]], the [[status-boards]] it publishes, and the [[event-management-gni|GNI wire]]
through which alarms open their own incidents. Integrations reach the personnel
databases and, through the GGUS bridge, the grid's own helpdesk.

The detail that closes the loop: ServiceNow **has its own entry in the catalogue it
runs** — a Functional Element with a supporting team, subject to the same incident and
change processes as everything it administers.
