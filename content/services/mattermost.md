# Mattermost

Adopted in early 2020 under [[malt]], replacing a proprietary collaboration app after
its subscription terms and data-handling changed. What makes it more than a chat client
at CERN is the wiring: integrated with close to **100 tools**, including Jira, GitLab
and GitHub — the open-source replacement ended up more embedded than the product it
replaced.
