# InvenioRDM

The engine CERN co-develops with 20+ partners (Northwestern among the core; live
instances at TU Graz, TU Wien, `repository.cern`): a turn-key research data management
repository claiming **"1 or 100 million records, 1 byte or several petabytes"**.
Institutions deploy their own sovereign instances and inherit DOI minting, versioning
and rich metadata.
