# Computing Resources Scrutiny Group

Established in **2007**, the CRSG exists to **inform the decisions of the C-RRB**: it
examines the CPU, disk and tape requests of ATLAS, CMS, ALICE and LHCb against their
actual utilisation across Tier-0, Tier-1 and Tier-2. Per the MoU, scrutiny starts from
the request information presented at the spring C-RRB meeting. It is the mechanism that
makes "we need more computing" an auditable claim.
