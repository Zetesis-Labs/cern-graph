# The Medium-Term Plan

The MTP is how the [[cern-council]] approves strategy and budget over a rolling
five-year horizon. For IT it is decisive: the construction of the
[[prevessin-computing-centre]] was funded through it, justified by CERN's obligations
as [[cern-tier0]] of the WLCG for Run 3 and beyond. Governance becomes concrete here —
this is where a committee decision becomes concrete and steel.
