# Computing Resources Review Board (C-RRB)

The board where the funding agencies of the LHC experiments review and decide computing
resources. It does not deliberate blind: the [[crsg]] scrutinises the requests first
and reports to it, and the process runs on a spring/autumn cycle anchored in the
[[wlcg-mou-agreement]].
