# CASTOR

The CERN Advanced STORage manager: the hierarchical system that managed archival
storage of physics data through **Runs 1 and 2**. Replaced by [[cta]], which is
described as its evolution rather than its rival — the migration of ATLAS's 83 PB is
what marked CTA's entry into production.
