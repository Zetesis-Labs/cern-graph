# CERNBox

CERN's cloud storage for people rather than experiments: store, share and synchronise
across devices, with web, desktop sync, mobile, WebDAV, SSHFS and CIFS/SMB access, plus
collaborative editing and Jupyter integration. Its interest architecturally is what it
stands on — **[[eos]]**, the same system that holds the physics data. One storage
substrate, two very different user populations.
