# GitLab adoption growing at CERN

GitLab's account of its deployment at CERN.

## What it anchors

* CERN hosts software projects and configuration management code on a self-hosted GitLab with around **2,500 active developers**, reaching more than 2,000 projects within months — far faster than CERN's previous code hosting platforms.
