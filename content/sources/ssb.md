# CERN Service Status Board

The Service Status Board.

## What it anchors

* A live view of ongoing incidents and issues for end users and supporters.
* Announcements carry one of **three types** — Service Incident, Planned Intervention, Service Change — and one of **three impact levels**: Down, Degraded or No Impact.
