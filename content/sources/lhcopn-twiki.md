# LHCOPN — LHC Optical Private Network

The LHCOPN/LHCONE working area.

## What it anchors

* **LHCOPN** links CERN to all the WLCG Tier-1s with dedicated connections for LHC data distribution.
* **LHCONE** extends the model to the Tier-2 community as an overlay service, implemented as an L3VPN with VRF, deployed by around 40 national, regional and intercontinental R&E network operators and connecting more than 150 sites on four continents.
