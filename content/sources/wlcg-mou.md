# WLCG Memorandum of Understanding

The sample WLCG Memorandum of Understanding published by the collaboration.

## What it anchors

* The MoU governs the deployment and exploitation of the WLCG: it defines the programme of work, the distribution of duties and responsibilities of the parties, and the **Computing Resource Levels** they offer to the LHC experiments.
* Annex 5 carries the terms of reference of the collaboration's bodies.
