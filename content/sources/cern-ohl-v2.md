# CERN Open Hardware Licence v2

The text of the CERN Open Hardware Licence version 2 (permissive variant), published on the Open Hardware Repository.

## What it anchors

* CERN developed the licence to support the freedom to use, study, modify, share and distribute hardware designs.
* **Version 2 (March 2020)** splits into three variants: **CERN-OHL-P** (permissive), **CERN-OHL-W** (weakly reciprocal) and **CERN-OHL-S** (strongly reciprocal); absent an explicit choice, covered source is treated as CERN-OHL-S.
* Its scope was broadened beyond electronics to mechanical and artistic designs, ASICs, FPGAs and even software.
