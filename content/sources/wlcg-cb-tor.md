# WLCG Collaboration Board

Terms of reference of the WLCG Collaboration Board.

## What it anchors

* Composed of the institutions that have signed the WLCG MoU — the collaboration's own assembly, distinct from the CERN Council.
* The Overview Board is a **standing committee of the Collaboration Board**.
