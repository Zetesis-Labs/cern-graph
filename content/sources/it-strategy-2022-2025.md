# IT Department Strategy 2022–2025

The IT Department's strategy document (CERN Document Server, record 2799153, January 2022).

## What it anchors

* The four roles of the department: **provider** (meet the IT needs of the Organization), **optimiser** (enhance production-quality services), **pioneer** (prepare for the evolution of the scientific programmes — HL-LHC, Physics Beyond Colliders, FCC), **connector** (increase CERN's scientific, societal and economic impact, in the spirit of Open Science).
* Cost-effective balance between standardised common services and specialised ones.
