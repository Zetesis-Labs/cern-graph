# CERN management structure for 2026–2030 (part 3)

Third installment of the series, on department-level appointments.

## What it anchors

* **Simone Campana** is appointed head of the Information Technology department.
* Department heads were selected through an open expression-of-interest call launched in late September 2025, with appointments in late October — "an open and transparent selection of management positions".
