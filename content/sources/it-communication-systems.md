# IT Communication Systems group

The IT department's own description of its Communication Systems group.

## What it anchors

* The network estate: a **site-wide campus network** with Wi-Fi and eduroam, a **high-performance data centre network**, high-bandwidth links to the worldwide physics community, and a **dedicated technical network** supporting accelerator operations plus control networks for the experiments.
* The group maintains **LanDB**, the central networking database, and the portal where users register their devices.
