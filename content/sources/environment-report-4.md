# CERN's 4th Environment Report

CERN's fourth environment report, covering 2023 and 2024.

## What it anchors

* In 2024 the Enlarged Directorate approved environmental objectives to 2030, **committing CERN to limit electricity consumption to 1.5 TWh/year during LHC Run 4**.
* CERN holds **ISO 50001** energy management certification; a decade of efficiency measures has cut consumption by about 10%.
