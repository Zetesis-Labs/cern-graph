# AARC Policy Development Kit

The AARC community's Policy Development Kit.

## What it anchors

* A modular set of policy templates that research infrastructures adopt instead of drafting their own, complementing the technical **AARC Blueprint Architecture**.
* The trust framework distinguishes stable high-level policies from adaptable operational procedures.
