# InvenioRDM — the turn-key research data management repository

The Invenio project's product page for InvenioRDM.

## What it anchors

* "Handle 1 or 100 million records, 1 byte or several petabytes."
* Over 20 partner organizations, with CERN and Northwestern University among the core; live instances at TU Graz, TU Wien, Northwestern (PRISM) and CERN (repository.cern).
* The distinction the corpus relies on: **Zenodo is a repository service built on InvenioRDM; InvenioRDM is the repository application** others deploy.
