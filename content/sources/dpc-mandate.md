# CERN Data Protection Commission

The page of CERN's data protection supervisory body.

## What it anchors

* The DPC is CERN's **independent data protection supervisory authority**, mandated to monitor compliance with OC11, ensure the application of data subject rights, and evaluate and investigate complaints.
