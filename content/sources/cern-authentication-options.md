# CERN SSO authentication options

CERN Authentication and Authorization Services documentation for supported sign-in
methods.

## What it anchors

* CERN account and Kerberos authentication for account holders.
* Two-factor authentication with an authenticator app or hardware token.
* eduGAIN, social and guest accounts for visiting users.
