# IT Department Vision and Objectives

The IT Department's public page restating the strategy pillars and listing its organisational groups.

## What it anchors

* The four pillars restated as IT-as-Provider / Optimizer / Pioneer / Connector, with HL-LHC and AI/quantum named among the pioneering challenges.
* The department's group structure (Collaborative Applications, Compute and Devices, Computing for Experiments, Databases and Analytics, Department Infrastructure, Fabric and Communication Systems, Platforms and Workflows, Resource and Programme Management, Storage and Data Management).
