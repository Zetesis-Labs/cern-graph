# IT Fabric and Communication Systems group

CERN IT's current description of the group responsible for core physical and
communications infrastructure.

## What it anchors

* Operation of the data centres and associated computing facilities.
* Hardware procurement and lifecycle management for server, storage and
  data-centre equipment.
* Campus, data-centre, external, experiment-control and technical networks.
* Essential network services including DNS, DHCP and NTP.
