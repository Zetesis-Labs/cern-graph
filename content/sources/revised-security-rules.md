# Revised computer security rules

Official news announcing the revision process of CERN's computing rules.

## What it anchors

* Subsidiary Rules are discussed and approved (or rejected) by the **Computer Security Board**, comprised of appointed Computer Security Liaisons representing CERN sectors, departments, units and the experiments.
* Successive board meetings approved the IAA, EPT/SWR, OPS/NET and DPP/DEV rule families.
