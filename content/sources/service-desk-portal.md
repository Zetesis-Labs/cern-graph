# CERN Service Desk

The Service Desk page on the CERN Service Portal.

## What it anchors

* One phone number (**+41 22 76 77777**), one address (service-desk@cern.ch) and one portal for every request, staffed **07:00–18:30 on working days** from Building 33.
* First-line support for CERN services, dispatching to the right Functional Element when first line cannot resolve.
