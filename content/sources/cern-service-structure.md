# CERN Service Structure — Service and Functional Elements

The CERN Service Management site's definition of the service structure.

## What it anchors

* **Service Elements** are the individual services users can request through the catalogue; their names "should reflect the functionality that can be expected from service provision", and **service targets and quality parameters are defined at this level**.
* **Functional Elements** are the "technical services" provided by the different Groups and Sections. **Every Service Element rests on one or more Functional Elements.**
* The dependency between them is **classified by criticality**:
  * **A (Essential)** — failure renders the service unavailable; where several exist, one is marked **A+**
  * **B (Important)** — failure impairs functionality and availability
  * **C (Necessary)** — provides input or support; failure does not directly affect availability
  * **X (Non-relevant)** — no specific assignment needed
* That classification is operational, not documentary: "The identification and selection of the correct functional element is required for priority definition and dispatching; if no specific workgroup can be identified the ticket is automatically dispatched to the workgroup associated with the A(+) functional element."
