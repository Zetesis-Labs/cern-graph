# Who's who in the CERN senior leadership team

The official roster of the 2026–2030 senior leadership team.

## What it anchors

* All five sector directors: Brüning (ATS), Hamel de Monchenault (RCS), Bassler (SRS), Brouwer (FHR), Capeáns (SO).
* **Enrica Porcari takes up the new role of Chief Information Officer**, "responsible for steering the Organization's information technology strategy" — a non-departmental role in the senior leadership team, the first in CERN's history.
