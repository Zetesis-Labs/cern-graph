# Migrating to open-source technologies

CERN's own account of the Microsoft Alternatives project.

## What it anchors

* The trigger: after twenty years on discounted "academic institution" rates, **Microsoft revoked CERN's academic status** and licensing costs rose more than tenfold.
* The project's **principles of engagement**: deliver the same service to every category of CERN personnel, avoid vendor lock-in to reduce risk and dependency, keep hands on the data, and address the common use cases.
* The replacement set, **Keycloak for SSO** among them.
