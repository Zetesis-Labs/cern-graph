# White Rabbit

The White Rabbit project site.

## What it anchors

* An **open hardware and software project** for sub-nanosecond synchronisation accuracy over Gigabit Ethernet, combining hardware timestamping, Synchronous Ethernet and phase detection.
* Started in 2009 by CERN, GSI and partners from universities and industry, to synchronise thousands of devices across accelerator facilities.
* It became the reference for the **High Accuracy profile of IEEE 1588-2019**, and is sold commercially by third parties.
