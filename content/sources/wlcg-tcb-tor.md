# WLCG Technical Coordination Board terms of reference

The WLCG page defining the board that took over technical coordination after the Grid
Deployment Board concluded.

## What it anchors

* The TCB is responsible for the technical evolution of WLCG services.
* It defines a multi-year roadmap and is responsible for implementing it.
* Its bottom-up Open Technical Forum welcomes contributors from across WLCG.
