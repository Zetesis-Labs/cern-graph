# CERN management structure for 2026–2030 (part 1)

First opinion piece by the incoming Director-General announcing the 2026–2030 directorate structure, endorsed at the March Council session.

## What it anchors

* Five sectors and 13 departments.
* ATS under Oliver Brüning with Beams (BE), Engineering (EN), Accelerator Systems (SY) and Technology (TE), entering LS3.
* Mar Capeáns leads the new Site Operations sector, "similar to that of a traditional Chief Operating Officer".
* FHR retains FAP, HR and Industry, Procurement and Knowledge Transfer (IPT).
