# Changing the CERN lightbulbs with ServiceNow

Diginomica's 2019 piece on CERN's organisation-wide service management.

## What it anchors

* The same processes that route an IT incident handle **failed lightbulbs, snow clearing, drainage, car parking and employee onboarding** — the catalogue covers the physical campus, not just computing.
* The insight is attributed to the organisation service management function: the ITIL model generalises to "most areas of service provision".
