# CERN Puppet-based configuration management (2024)

A CERN IT role notice documenting the production service at that date.

## What it anchors

* IT operated a Puppet-based configuration-management service in 2024.
* About 14,000 Linux and Windows servers relied on that service at the time.
* The source does not establish that Foreman remained part of the current
  architecture.
