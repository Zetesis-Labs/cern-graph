# IT Service Management at CERN

CHEP paper on service management at CERN (CERN Document Server, record 2698991).

## What it anchors

* CERN has used **ITIL** service management methodologies and **ServiceNow** since early 2011.
* The service catalogue is **two-dimensional**: a **Service Element (SE)** describes the service as users see it, and a **Functional Element (FE)** describes the tool, function or activity supporting it — with supporters and experts declared at FE level.
* The Service Portal hosts the Status Board and the Service Availability Dashboard.
