# A modern telephony system at CERN

CERN's announcement of the migration to CERNphone (February 2022).

## What it anchors

* CERNphone replaced the fixed phones on the **ageing Alcatel PABX**, alongside **TONE**, developed in-house to take over the exchange's functions.
* Available for Android and iOS, and for Windows, macOS and Linux desktops.
* Migration ran progressively through 2022, scheduled to complete by mid-2023.
