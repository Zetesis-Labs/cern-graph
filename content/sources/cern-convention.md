# Convention for the Establishment of CERN

The founding treaty of the Organization (1953), published by the CERN Council.

## What it anchors

* The Council as the supreme governing organ, composed of delegates of the Member States — Article IV–V.
* The Scientific Policy Committee and the Finance Committee as mandatory subordinate bodies, plus any others the Council creates by a two-thirds majority — Article V, paragraph 12.
