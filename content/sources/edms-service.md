# EDMS — CERN's Engineering Data Management Service

The EDMS service site.

## What it anchors

* Established in the 1990s for the LHC project, EDMS stores, manages and distributes engineering information, with document life cycles, review processes, versioning, project numbering and access rights.
* It is built on commercial PLM and EAM products and integrated with the CAD and maintenance-management systems, with CERN-developed web applications on top.
