# Service management at CERN with Service-Now (CHEP 2012)

Toteva et al., J. Phys.: Conf. Ser. 396 062022.

## What it anchors

* Service management arrived as a **joint project of the General Services and IT departments** — organisation-wide from day one, not an IT tool that later escaped.
* The **two-dimensional service catalogue** combining the user view and the support-team view preceded the tool: Service-Now was selected to implement it.
* First processes live: **incident management and request fulfilment**, with a deliberately simplified request process for user adoption; change management and the facility-management integration followed.
* Integrations from the start: **CERN personnel databases and the LHC Grid ticketing systems**.
