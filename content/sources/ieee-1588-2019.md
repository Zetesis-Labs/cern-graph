# IEEE 1588-2019

The IEEE Standard for a Precision Clock Synchronization Protocol for Networked Measurement and Control Systems.

## What it anchors

* The standardisation of the High Accuracy profile, which directly incorporates the White Rabbit technology developed at CERN to achieve sub-nanosecond accuracy over Gigabit Ethernet.
