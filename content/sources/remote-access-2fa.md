# Computer Security: 5 ways to remotely connect to CERN

The Computer Security team's article on remote access hardening.

## What it anchors

* 2FA deployed to the CERN SSO in 2023–2024, and **activated on Windows Terminal Services (RDP) and LXPLUS (SSH) in September 2025**, the latter via a PAM module.
* The rollout fulfils a recommendation of the 2023 cybersecurity audit; supported factors include smartphone apps, YubiKeys and fingerprint readers.
