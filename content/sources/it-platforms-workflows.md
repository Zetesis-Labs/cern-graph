# IT Platforms and Workflows group

CERN IT's current description of the group responsible for platforms and
workflows.

## What it anchors

* Application hosting on OpenShift and other platforms.
* Hosting of EDH and EDMS among business and engineering applications.
* Web hosting, analytics, Drupal and WordPress services.
* CERN SSO, WLCG IAM, GMS and lifecycle management for accounts, applications
  and groups.
