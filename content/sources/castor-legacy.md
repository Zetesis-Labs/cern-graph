# CASTOR — CERN Advanced STORage manager

The CASTOR project site.

## What it anchors

* CASTOR managed the archival storage of physics data during **Runs 1 and 2** of the LHC.
* It was succeeded by CTA, which is described as its evolution; the migration of ATLAS data — 83 PB — marked CTA's entry into production.
