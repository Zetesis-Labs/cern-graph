# CERN management structure for 2026–2030 (part 2)

Second installment of the series.

## What it anchors

* The Research and Computing Sector (RCS) consists of Experimental Physics (EP), Theoretical Physics (TH) and **Information Technology (IT)**, under Gautier Hamel de Monchenault.
* Ursula Bassler leads Stakeholder Relations; Jan-Paul Brouwer leads Finance and Human Resources.
