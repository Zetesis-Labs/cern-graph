# The Large Hadron Collider and its experiments

CERN's public description of the LHC and its experiments.

## What it anchors

* The four principal LHC collision points host ATLAS, CMS, ALICE and LHCb.
* These experiments generate the data processed and distributed through the
  worldwide LHC computing infrastructure.
