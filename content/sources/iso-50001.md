# ISO 50001:2018

The international standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining and improving an energy management system.

## What it anchors

* The certification framework that CERN uses to monitor, manage, and commit to its energy consumption targets, such as the 1.5 TWh/year limit for LHC Run 4.
