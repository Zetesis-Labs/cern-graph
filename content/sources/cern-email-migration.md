# Migration of the CERN e-mail system to open source

CERN IT presentation on the e-mail migration (Indico).

## What it anchors

* The target of the migration: around **40,000 mailboxes and 50–60 TB of mail data**, moving from Microsoft Exchange to the open-source **Kopano**, chosen after market research, evaluation and proofs of concept, with a support contract with the vendor.
* It was among the first tangible outcomes of MALT.
