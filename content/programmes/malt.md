# MALT — Microsoft Alternatives

After twenty years on discounted academic rates, **Microsoft revoked CERN's academic
status** and licensing costs rose **more than tenfold**. MALT was the answer, and its
principles of engagement are the doctrine behind much of the current architecture:
deliver the same service to *every* category of personnel, **avoid vendor lock-in** to
reduce risk and dependency, keep hands on the data, and address the common use cases.
Keycloak entered CERN through this door.
