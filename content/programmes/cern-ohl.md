# CERN Open Hardware Licence

A licence drafted at CERN to give hardware designs what free software already had.
**Version 2 (March 2020)** splits into three variants — **CERN-OHL-P** (permissive),
**CERN-OHL-W** (weakly reciprocal) and **CERN-OHL-S** (strongly reciprocal), the
default when none is specified — and broadens scope beyond electronics to mechanical
and artistic designs, ASICs, FPGAs and even software. A research laboratory legislating
in intellectual property because no existing instrument fit.
