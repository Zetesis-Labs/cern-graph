# LHCONE

The LHC Open Network Environment connects the Tier-1s and Tier-2s as an **L3VPN
implemented with VRF** — a dedicated worldwide backbone reserved for HEP data transfers
and analysis. Deployed by around **40 national, regional and intercontinental R&E
network operators**, connecting more than 150 sites on four continents. Governance by
federation applies to the wires too.
