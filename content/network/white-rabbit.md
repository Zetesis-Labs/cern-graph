# White Rabbit

Not a data network: a **timing** network. Started in 2009 by CERN, GSI and partners to
synchronise thousands of devices across accelerator facilities, White Rabbit extends
Precision Time Protocol with hardware timestamping, Synchronous Ethernet and phase
detection to reach **sub-nanosecond accuracy over Gigabit Ethernet**. Released as
**open hardware**, it became the reference for the High Accuracy profile of
**IEEE 1588-2019** and is now sold by commercial vendors — the clearest case of the
open-hardware strategy paying off outside the laboratory.
