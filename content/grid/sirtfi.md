# Sirtfi — federated incident response

Federated identity creates a jurisdictional gap: the institution that issued the
credential and the site where it is abused may share no authority, no contract and no
language. **Sirtfi** closes it by having participants commit to a baseline of incident
response capability and contactability. It is critical for handling incidents involving
CERN and the WLCG — the security counterpart to [[vo-transitive-trust]].
