# GGUS

The WLCG does not use CERN's helpdesk: it has its own. **GGUS**, developed and operated
at KIT, has been the grid's central ticketing system **since 2007** — a German lab
running the support desk for a worldwide federation. The seam with CERN is the
**GGUS–SNow interface**: tickets for CERN grid services cross the bridge and are worked
inside [[servicenow]] under its own priorities. After more than 20 years, GGUS is being
replaced by a next-generation helpdesk — a succession in progress.
