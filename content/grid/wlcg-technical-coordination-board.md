# WLCG Technical Coordination Board

The **Technical Coordination Board (TCB)** steers the technical evolution of WLCG
services according to experiment needs and infrastructure-provider capabilities. It
defines and implements a multi-year roadmap through a bottom-up **Open Technical
Forum**, where contributors from across the WLCG community can participate.
