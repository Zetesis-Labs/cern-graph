# WLCG Management Board

Where the work is actually organised: it maintains the overall programme of work and
the planning data. Its composition is a map of the collaboration's power — the WLCG
Leader and deputies, representatives of the **Tier-0 and Tier-1 centres**, each LHC
experiment's Computing Coordinator, service and operations coordinators, and the
chairs of the [[wlcg-technical-coordination-board]].
