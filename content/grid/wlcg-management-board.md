# WLCG Management Board

Where the work is actually organised: it maintains the overall programme of work and
the planning data. Its composition is a map of the collaboration's power — the Project
Leader, the **Technical Heads of the Tier-1 centres**, the activity leaders, the
Computing Coordinator of each LHC experiment, and the chair of the [[wlcg-gdb]].
