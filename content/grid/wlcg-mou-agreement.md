# The WLCG Memorandum of Understanding

The grid runs on a signed commitment. The MoU defines the programme of work, the
distribution of duties and responsibilities between the parties, and — the operative
part — the **Computing Resource Levels** each party offers to the LHC experiments.
Its Annex 5 carries the terms of reference of every governing body. It is what makes
a promise of CPU in another continent enforceable.
