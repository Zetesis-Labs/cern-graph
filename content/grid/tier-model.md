# The tier model

The architecture that makes the grid a federation rather than a data centre: **Tier-0**
keeps the first copy of the raw data, does first-pass reconstruction and distributes;
**fifteen Tier-1s** hold proportional shares with round-the-clock support and do
large-scale reprocessing; **Tier-2s** are where analysis and simulation happen; and
smaller **Tier-3** resources are controlled by local institutes or individual groups.
Across ~170 centres in 40+ countries: ~3 exabytes and about a million cores — of which
CERN provides only around **20% of the compute**. The number is the argument for
federated governance.
