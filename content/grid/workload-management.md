# WLCG Workload Management

> [!warning] Primary source needed
> The function is retained as a research lead. The current experiment-by-experiment
> products, interfaces and data-management dependencies need primary sources.

LHC experiment workload-management systems distribute analysis and production jobs
across WLCG sites. They are modelled under the grid federation, not as a WLCG
governance body, and no universal dependency on CERN's local batch service or Rucio
is asserted here.
