# IT Department

The department that runs the machines. Led since 2026 by **Simone Campana**, appointed
through an open expression-of-interest call — and organized in specialised groups
(compute, storage, databases, platforms, networks). It operates the identity stack,
hosts the WLCG IAM instances, and carries the [[it-strategy-pillars]] into production.
