# E-groups (–2026)

The legacy group system: static lists, maintained by hand, feeding access control and
mail alike. Groups were transferred automatically to [[gms]] between July and September
2025; e-groups went read-only in Q2 2026 and is being decommissioned. Its API clients
have until the end of September 2026.
