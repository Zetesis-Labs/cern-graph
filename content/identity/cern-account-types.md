# CERN Account Types

CERN distinguishes Primary accounts (one main login per affiliated person),
Secondary accounts (alternative personal logins) and Service accounts (logins for
services or automation). Users without a CERN affiliation can use guest or linked
external identities. The lifecycle differs: personal accounts are eventually
blocked and deleted after departure, while Service accounts can be transferred.
