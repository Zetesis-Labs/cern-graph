# CERN Council and Governance Committees

The **CERN Council** is the supreme governing body of the European Organization for Nuclear Research, composed of delegates from its 25 Member States. It holds ultimate responsibility for all scientific, technical and administrative decisions, including the review of expenditure and budget approval for the data centre infrastructure.

---

## 🏛️ Subordinate Committees and Oversight

The Council delegates responsibilities to three key governance committees:

1. **Scientific Policy Committee (SPC)**:
   * Assesses the merit and technical feasibility of research and accelerator projects.
2. **Finance Committee**:
   * Oversees public tenders and contract awards for the procurement of data centre hardware.
3. **Standing Advisory Committee on Audits (SACA)**:
   * Subordinate body established by the Council under Article V, paragraph 12, of the CERN Convention.
   * Advises the Council on the effectiveness of the Organization's audit structure —including that of its Pension Fund— and on measures to mitigate the identified risks.
   * Its formal remit covers external audit, the independence of internal audit, certification of the financial statements, and follow-up on management's responses to recommendations.
   * The [[cis-v8-zero-trust|cybersecurity audit]] falls within the five-year internal audit plan, whose recommendations were accepted by the Director-General.
