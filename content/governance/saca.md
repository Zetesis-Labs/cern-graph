# SACA — the audit advisory committee

SACA advises the Council on the effectiveness of the Organization's audit structure —
including the Pension Fund's — and on mitigating identified risks. Its scope is
auditing machinery: external audit, internal audit independence, financial statements,
follow-up of recommendations. **Cybersecurity, business continuity and disaster
recovery are not in its terms of reference** — a common misattribution.
