# Executive Restructuring (2026-2030) and the CIO Role

Anticipating the massive computing challenges of the **High-Luminosity LHC (HL-LHC)**, the [[consejo-cern]] endorsed at its March 2025 session a deep reorganization structured into 5 Sectoral Directorates and 13 departments. The structure **has been in effect since January 2026**, coinciding with the assumption of office by Director General **Mark Thomson**.

## 👑 Institutionalization of the Chief Information Officer (CIO)

The most significant architectural milestone is the creation of the **Chief Information Officer (CIO)** position, placed outside the standard operational hierarchy of the IT Department. **Enrica Porcari** —until then head of the IT Department— assumed it in January 2026 as the first CIO in the laboratory's history, integrated into the senior executive team.

### Governance Implications
* **Separation of Powers**: The CIO sets strategic regulations on cybersecurity, data privacy, and Artificial Intelligence policies at the executive level.
* **Decoupled Operation**: The IT Department (nested within the RCS sector and led by **Simone Campana**) executes and operates the technical infrastructure under the CIO's directives.
* **Transparent Selection**: Department heads in this structure were appointed through an open call for expressions of interest launched in late September 2025, with appointments in October.
