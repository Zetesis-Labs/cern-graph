# The five-sector structure (2026–2030)

The 2026–2030 reorganization: **five sectors and 13 departments**, endorsed by the
Council in March 2025 and in force since January 2026 with Mark Thomson as
Director-General. ATS (Brüning) keeps the accelerator chain stable through LS3;
Site Operations (Capeáns) plays the COO; FHR (Brouwer) holds finance and people;
SRS (Bassler) faces the Member States; and [[rcs-sector]] holds the science — and IT.
