# The CIO role

In January 2026 Enrica Porcari became CERN's **first-ever CIO** — a non-departmental
role in the senior leadership team, "responsible for steering the Organization's
information technology strategy". The separation is the point: the CIO sets the norms
(cybersecurity, data privacy, AI), while [[it-department]] operates the systems under
them. Strategy stops competing with uptime for the same person's attention.
