# Data Protection Commission

The DPC monitors compliance with [[oc11]], ensures the application of data subject
rights, and evaluates and investigates complaints. It is CERN's own answer to the
supervisory authority that national data protection law would otherwise provide — the
institutional consequence of being outside national jurisdiction.
