# LHCOPN

The LHC Optical Private Network: dedicated connections linking CERN to **all** the WLCG
Tier-1 centres for data distribution. It exists because the Tier-0→Tier-1 flow is too
large, too continuous and too important to compete for capacity on general-purpose
research networks.
