# HR Database Identity Lifecycle

Most identity information in CERN's Authorization Service is read-only and
synchronized from HR's Foundation database. Account ownership and resource
categories then support lifecycle decisions. External, guest and service identities
mean that not every account can be described simply as one HR personnel record.
