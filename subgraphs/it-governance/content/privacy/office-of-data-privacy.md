# Office of Data Privacy

The unit that advises data subjects, CERN personnel and external entities involved in
processing personal data, headed by the **Data Privacy Adviser**. It is the expertise
arm — distinct from the [[data-protection-commission]], which is the supervisory one.
The separation matters: whoever advises on compliance cannot also judge it.
