# OC11 — the processing of personal data

CERN's **internal data protection framework**. Revision 1 entered into force on
**1 February 2026**, replacing the 2019 circular and its annexes, and clarifying the
conditions for consent, the [[dpia]] procedure, the role of external entities, CERN's
position as data controller, and data subject rights. As an intergovernmental
organization CERN is not bound by the GDPR — so it legislates its own equivalent.
