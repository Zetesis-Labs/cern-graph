# The Open Science Policy (2022)

The mandate over the output: papers open access, experimental data released (CC0 as
standard), software **and hardware** open source, plus research integrity, open
infrastructure and citizen science — drafted by a working group spanning every
department and approved with immediate effect in September 2022.
