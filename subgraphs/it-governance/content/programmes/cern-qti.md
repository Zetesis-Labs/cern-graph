# CERN Quantum Technology Initiative

QTI's governance is deliberately two-layered: a **Coordination Team** of CERN experts
led by a Coordinator sets priorities, while an **Advisory Board** of quantum experts
from the Member States provides oversight. The design goal is stated openly — maximise
the integration of bottom-up innovation with top-down guidance, the standing problem of
governing an emerging technology nobody yet knows how to plan.
