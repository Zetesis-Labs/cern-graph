# CERN openlab

The instrument through which CERN co-develops with leading ICT companies and research
institutes, giving them access to its infrastructure and engineering experience in
exchange for early work on technologies it will need. Structured in **three-year
phases**, each reassessing priorities — a cadence that keeps a partnership from
fossilising into a procurement channel. It is the *Connector* pillar made concrete.
