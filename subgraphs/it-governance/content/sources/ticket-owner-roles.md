# CERN Service Management — Ticket Owner and roles

The role definitions of CERN's service management framework.

## What it anchors

* "The Ticket Owner is **accountable for the eventual successful closure** of all Incidents and Service Requests" — normally first line, but whoever opens a ticket at second or third line assumes it.
* The role exists to stop tickets "being passed back and forth between different groups".
* The surrounding roles: Service Owner, Functional Service Manager, Service Manager, Service Desk Manager, and the 1st/2nd/3rd line tiers.
