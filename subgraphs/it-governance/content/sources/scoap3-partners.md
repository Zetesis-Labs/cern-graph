# SCOAP3 partners and governance

The consortium's current register and description of its partnership model.

## What it anchors

* SCOAP3 comprises more than 3,000 participating organisations across 47 countries, regions or territories.
* CERN acts as Host Organisation.
* Representatives sign the memorandum that forms the basis of the partnership.
