# CERN IAM services FAQ (EGI)

EGI's operational FAQ describing the production IAM services run by CERN.

## What it anchors

* The CERN IAM services are **INDIGO-IAM instances for the LHC experiments and other CERN-based VOs**, hosted by CERN IT.
* Their purposes: VO user/group/role management, provision of WLCG tokens for user and service workflows, and provision of VOMS proxies while still needed.
