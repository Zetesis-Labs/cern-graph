# Operational Circular No. 11 (Rev.1) — Processing of Personal Data

The official announcement of the revised OC11.

## What it anchors

* OC11 constitutes CERN's **internal data protection framework**; Rev.1 entered into force on **1 February 2026**, cancelling and replacing the January 2019 circular and its annexes.
* The revision clarifies conditions for consent, the procedure for a **Data Privacy Impact Assessment (DPIA)**, the role of external entities, CERN's position as data controller, and data subject rights.
