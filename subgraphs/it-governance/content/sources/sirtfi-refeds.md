# Sirtfi — Security Incident Response Trust Framework

The REFEDS specification of Sirtfi.

## What it anchors

* Sirtfi exists to **enable the coordination of incident response across federated organisations** — the missing piece when an identity is issued by one institution and abused at another.
* It is critical for handling incidents involving CERN and the WLCG efficiently.
