# CERN Open Science Policy (CERN-OPEN-2022-013)

The Organization-wide open science policy (CERN-OPEN-2022-013, October 2022 publication of the policy approved in September 2022).

## What it anchors

* Approved September 2022 **with immediate effect**, developed by the Open Science Strategy Working Group with members from every department.
* Encompasses open access, open data, open source software **and hardware**, research integrity, open infrastructure, research assessment, training and citizen science.
* CC0 waivers as standard for experimental data releases.
