# Indico passes one million events

CERN's announcement on Indico's milestone, alongside a joint CERN–UN workshop.

## What it anchors

* Indico is used by **over 200 organisations including 11 UN agencies**; the United Nations have handled more than 180,000 conference participants with it since 2016.
* Launched at CERN in 2002 as a European project and adopted as CERN's own event-management solution in 2004.
