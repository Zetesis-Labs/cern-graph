# CERN Medium-Term Plan 2025–2029

The Medium-Term Plan and draft budget submitted to the Council (CERN Document Server).

## What it anchors

* The MTP is the instrument through which the **Council approves the Organization's strategy and budget** over a rolling five-year period.
* It is where computing infrastructure is funded — including the construction of the Prévessin Computing Centre, needed to meet CERN's obligations as **Tier-0 of the WLCG**.
