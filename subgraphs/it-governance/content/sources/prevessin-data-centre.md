# A new data centre at CERN

CERN's announcement of the Prévessin Computing Centre.

## What it anchors

* Inaugurated **23 February 2024** on the Prévessin site (France), spanning more than 6000 m², with six IT rooms of 2 MW cooling capacity each.
* Target **PUE of 1.1**, against a worldwide average around 1.6, with a heat-recovery system set to warm buildings on the site.
