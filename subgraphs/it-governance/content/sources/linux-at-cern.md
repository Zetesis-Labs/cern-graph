# Linux @ CERN — migrations

The CERN Linux service's migration guidance.

## What it anchors

* **CC7 reached end of life on 30 June 2024**. Through a special agreement with the Accelerator and Technology Sector, CERN IT rebuilds Red Hat's Extended Lifecycle Support to be consumable at CERN **until 1 January 2027**.
* **CERN and Fermilab jointly plan to provide AlmaLinux** as the standard distribution for experiments at their facilities.
* In-place upgrades are not recommended: the procedure is to install a new machine with the new OS.
