# WLCG Overview Board

Terms of reference of the WLCG Overview Board.

## What it anchors

* Its role: overseeing the functioning of the WLCG Collaboration and **of the MoU in particular**.
* It includes Tier-2 representatives to deal specifically with Tier-2 issues.
