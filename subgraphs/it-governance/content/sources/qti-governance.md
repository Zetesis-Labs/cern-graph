# CERN QTI — our governance

The governance page of the CERN Quantum Technology Initiative.

## What it anchors

* A structure designed to combine **bottom-up participation with top-down guidance**: a Coordination Team of CERN experts led by a Coordinator sets priorities, while an **Advisory Board** of quantum experts from Member States provides oversight.
