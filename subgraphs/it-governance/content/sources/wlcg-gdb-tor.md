# WLCG Grid Deployment Board (concluded Sept 2024)

Terms of reference of the Grid Deployment Board, whose own page records that it **concluded in September 2024**.

## What it anchors

* The GDB represented in particular the **resource centres** of the collaboration.
* Its chair was an ex officio member of the Management Board, where he represented the interests of those centres.
* Its conclusion in September 2024 — the corpus's clearest case of a governance body that no longer exists.
