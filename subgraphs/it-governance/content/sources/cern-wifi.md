# CERN Wi-Fi network documentation

The Wi-Fi service documentation.

## What it anchors

* **CERN-Campus** is the encrypted general-purpose wireless network being rolled out across the site, alongside **eduroam** for visiting researchers.
* Devices are registered through the same network portal that backs onto LanDB.
