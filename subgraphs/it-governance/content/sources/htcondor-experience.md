# HTCondor: Distributed Computing Experience

A foundational peer-reviewed paper describing the architecture, mechanisms, and design philosophy of the HTCondor high-throughput computing system.

## What it anchors

* The system architecture and capabilities that enabled CERN to replace LSF and scale its batch service to handle the massive high-throughput workload of physics jobs.
