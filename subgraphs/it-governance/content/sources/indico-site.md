# Indico

The Indico project site.

It serves as the main distribution and technical hub for the open-source event management platform developed at CERN. The site details Indico's capabilities across the complete event lifecycle, including agenda creation, registration, and document submission. It also highlights the project's external community adoption by over 200 institutions worldwide, aligning with CERN's [[open-science-mandate]].

## What it anchors

* Indico manages the complete lifecycle of events, from a simple meeting to a large international conference; it is open source and developed at CERN.
