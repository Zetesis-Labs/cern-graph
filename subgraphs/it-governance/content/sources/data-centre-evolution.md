# Powering the future of data science at CERN

CERN's update on its two computing centres in the run-up to High-Luminosity LHC.

## What it anchors

* Meyrin is receiving electrical-distribution, rack, cooling and dual-power-feed
  resilience improvements.
* Prévessin is expanding from 4 MW towards 12 MW, with heat recovery and cooling
  improvements.
