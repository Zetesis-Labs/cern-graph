# WLCG Computing Resources Scrutiny Group

Terms of reference of the CRSG.

## What it anchors

* Its purpose: **inform the decisions of the Computing Resources Review Board (C-RRB)** for the LHC experiments.
* Established in 2007; it reviews CPU, disk and tape requests and utilisation across Tier-0, Tier-1 and Tier-2 for ATLAS, CMS, ALICE and LHCb.
* Per the MoU, scrutiny starts from the request information presented at the spring C-RRB meeting.
