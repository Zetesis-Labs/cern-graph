# CERN openlab — our history

CERN openlab's account of its own trajectory.

## What it anchors

* A **public-private partnership** through which CERN collaborates with leading ICT companies and research institutes, giving them access to its infrastructure and engineering experience.
* Organised in **three-year phases**, each reassessing technological priorities; historically structured into competence centres with the partner companies.
