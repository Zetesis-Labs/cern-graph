# CERN and Mattermost

Mattermost's account of its deployment at CERN.

## What it anchors

* CERN adopted the open source messaging platform **in early 2020 under MALT**, replacing a proprietary collaboration app after subscription and data-handling changes.
* It has been integrated with close to **100 tools**, including Jira, GitLab and GitHub.
