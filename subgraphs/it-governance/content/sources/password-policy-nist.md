# Computer Security: ThisIsAVeryGoodPassword

The Computer Security team's article on the password policy revision.

## What it anchors

* The Computing Rules were updated in line with **NIST 800-63b**: complexity requirements dropped, minimum length extended to **15 characters**.
* New passwords are checked against lists of simple and "burnt" (breach-exposed) passwords; non-compliant passwords across all account types must be changed in 2026.
