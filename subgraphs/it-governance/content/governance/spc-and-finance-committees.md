# Scientific Policy and Finance Committees

The Convention does not let the Council govern alone: it **must** establish a Scientific
Policy Committee (scientific merit) and a Finance Committee (contributions, budget,
procurement — including data-centre tenders). Other subordinate bodies require a
two-thirds majority of Member States.
