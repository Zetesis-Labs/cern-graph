# Research and Computing Sector (RCS)

RCS bundles Experimental Physics, Theoretical Physics and **Information Technology**
under Gautier Hamel de Monchenault. Placing IT beside the experiments is a statement:
infrastructure provision answers directly to the computational demands of the physics
programme, not to an administrative branch.
