# CERN Control Centre

The **CERN Control Centre (CCC)** is the physical operations hub from which CERN
operates its accelerators, cryogenic systems and technical infrastructure around the
clock. It is a useful physical boundary for the graph's technical-network and
continuity-of-operations stories, rather than a location for individual network assets.
