# CERN as Tier-0

Tier-0 is a **duty**, not a privilege: safe-keeping of the raw data's first copy,
first-pass reconstruction, distribution to the Tier-1s and reprocessing during LHC
down-times. It is the commitment that justifies the capital expenditure — the
[[prevessin-computing-centre]] exists to meet it.
