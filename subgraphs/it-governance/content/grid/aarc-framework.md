# The AARC framework

AARC gives research infrastructures a **Blueprint Architecture** (technical) and a
**Policy Development Kit** (legal-operational): reusable templates so that every
collaboration does not draft its own trust framework from zero. Its modular design
separates stable high-level policies from adaptable operational procedures — the same
instinct as OC5 and its subsidiary rules, applied across institutions.
