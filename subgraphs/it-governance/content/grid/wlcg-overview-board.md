# WLCG Overview Board

A standing committee of the Collaboration Board, charged with overseeing the
functioning of the collaboration and **of the MoU in particular** — that is, whether
the pledges are being met. It includes Tier-2 representatives so that the smaller
centres are not spoken for by the large ones.
