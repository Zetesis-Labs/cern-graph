# WLCG Storage Federation

> [!warning] Primary source needed
> The concept is retained, but product-to-site assignments and claims of a single
> common namespace have not yet been verified against a current primary source.

WLCG uses heterogeneous storage services distributed across its tiered sites.
Rucio, FTS and XRootD have distinct data-management, transfer and access roles; this
node does not treat them as interchangeable or universal at every site.
