# EDMS

The Engineering Data Management Service, established in the 1990s for the LHC: it
stores, manages and distributes engineering information with document life cycles,
review processes, versioning, project numbering and access rights, integrated with the
CAD and maintenance-management systems. Its governing constraint is unusual —
**project lifecycles of 25 to 40 years** — which makes format longevity and controlled
documentation a design requirement, not good practice.
