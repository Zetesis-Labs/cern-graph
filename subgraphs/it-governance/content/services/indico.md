# Indico

Software written at CERN to manage the full lifecycle of an event, from a two-person
meeting to an international conference. Launched in 2002 as a European project and
adopted as CERN's own in 2004, it is today used by **over 200 organisations including
11 UN agencies** — the UN alone has handled more than 180,000 participants with it
since 2016. After the web itself, it is CERN's most widely adopted piece of software.
