# The Linux service

The operating system under the batch farm, lxplus and most of the data centre.
**CC7 reached end of life on 30 June 2024**, and CERN rebuilds Red Hat's Extended
Lifecycle Support internally — by special agreement with the Accelerator and Technology
Sector — **until 1 January 2027**, because accelerator systems cannot be rebuilt on
somebody else's schedule. The strategic move: **CERN and Fermilab jointly adopted
AlmaLinux** as the standard distribution for experiments at both laboratories, which
turns a distribution choice into an inter-laboratory agreement.
