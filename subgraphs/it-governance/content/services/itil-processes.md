# The ITIL processes

The user meets exactly two: **Incident Management** (something is broken) and
**Request Fulfilment** (I need something) — the latter deliberately **simplified** from
ITIL orthodoxy to win adoption. Behind them: Change, Event, Problem, Service Level and
Supplier Management. The founding paper's lesson survives in the design: processes were
chosen and trimmed for the population that would actually use them, not implemented
by the book.
