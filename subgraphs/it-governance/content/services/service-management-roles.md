# The accountability model

The rule that keeps 200 support groups from becoming a maze: "the **Ticket Owner** is
accountable for the eventual successful closure of all Incidents and Service
Requests" — normally first line, but whoever opens a ticket at second or third line
assumes the role. It exists to stop tickets "being passed back and forth between
different groups". Around it: Service Owner, Functional Service Manager, Service
Manager, Service Desk Manager, and the three support lines.
