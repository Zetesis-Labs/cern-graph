# The status boards

Two complementary faces: the **Service Status Board** carries human-written
announcements — each one of three types (Service Incident, Planned Intervention,
Service Change) and three impact levels (**Down, Degraded, No Impact**) — while the
**Service Availability Dashboard** (born as Service Level Status, hence `cern.ch/sls`)
shows the technical status computed from monitoring. Opinion and telemetry, kept apart
on purpose.
