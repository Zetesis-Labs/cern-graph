# The Alcatel PABX and Skype for Business

What CERNphone replaced: the **Alcatel IP phones** deployed between 2006 and 2014 on an
ageing private exchange, and **Skype for Business**, in service from 2013 to 2021.
Retiring both at once is the point — the laboratory had ended up running two parallel
telephony systems, one proprietary hardware, one proprietary cloud.
